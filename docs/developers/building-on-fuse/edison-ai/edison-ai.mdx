Fuse AI is Fuse's native infrastructure layer for agentic payments, enabling AI agents and automated systems to transact, settle, and interact with the Fuse ecosystem programmatically. Built on the same high-throughput, low-cost rails that power Fuse's consumer and business applications, Fuse AI is designed for a world where on-chain economic activity is increasingly driven by agents, not humans.

AI agents are becoming economic actors. They transact, hire, and settle work autonomously, but they cannot open bank accounts, satisfy KYC requirements, or hold a credit card. They can own a wallet. Fuse provides the settlement infrastructure that makes that wallet useful: sub-2s finality, transaction costs in fractions of a cent, and native support for the emerging standards that make agentic commerce interoperable at scale.

With Fuse AI, developers and builders can:

* Deploy agent wallets programmatically: Spin up ERC-4337 smart accounts for AI agents with no manual onboarding, no KYC friction, and full programmability via the FuseBox SDK.
* Enable machine-to-machine payments: Use Fuse's stablecoin rails to settle micropayments between agents, invoices, task completions,API calls, at the speed and cost that make agent-to-agent commerce economically viable.
* Integrate across agentic payment standards: Fuse natively supports the emerging protocol stack for agentic commerce: x402 for HTTP-native micropayments, MPP for machine-to-machine transactions, UCP as a universal commerce protocol, ACP as the checkout layer inside AI interfaces, and ERC-8183, the Ethereum standard for agentic commerce that Fuse is actively implementing.
* Access Fuse infrastructure via MCP: A dedicated Fuse MCP (Model Context Protocol) server is in development, exposing Fuse's payment and account infrastructure to any AI tool, agent framework, or application built on the network.
